Close-up rendering update:
The render derivative optimus-head-smooth-v2.glb refines curved patches with Phong interpolation.
All original control positions and axis bounds are retained; intermediate surface points are added.
MSAA/SMAA and studio material roughness improve the displayed contour.
The derivative is compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression; runtime decoding is losslessly verified.
